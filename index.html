A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Technology should work for people, not make life harder.

My journey into tech was not a straight line. I discovered software development while attending NIIT, later studied Information Technology at Greenfield University, and graduated with a First Class degree.

Building projects—especially VOTCAS, a university e-voting system—made me think beyond whether an application works. I became interested in whether people can understand it, trust it, and use it with confidence. That is why I'm especially interested in software development, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and human-centred technology.

I enjoy learning, building, improving, and sharing what I learn with others.
