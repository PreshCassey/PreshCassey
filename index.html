A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Technology should work for people, not make life harder.

My journey into tech wasn't a straight line. I discovered software development while studying at NIIT, which sparked an interest that later led me to pursue a degree in Information Technology at Greenfield University, where I graduated with First Class Honours.

Building projects, especially VOTCAS, a university e-voting system, strengthened my interest in creating practical software solutions. I enjoy exploring how technology can solve real-world problems, improve processes, and make everyday tasks easier.

I'm particularly interested in software development, financial technology (FinTech), digital innovation, and building technology-driven solutions that create meaningful impact.

I enjoy learning, experimenting with new tools, building ideas, and continuously improving my skills.
